Your standup, already written.

Sill reads the work you already do — your git commits, the tasks you finish, the follow-ups you send — and turns it into a standup and worklog you can paste in one tap. No logging. No discipline required.

Receipts for every blocker

Track what you're waiting on — an API, a design, a review, access — and log every follow-up on Slack, email, chat, a call or at standup. Sill shows how long you've waited and how many times you asked, and the receipt travels with your standup. A slow dependency is never on you.

Your day, writing itself down

  • A calm timeline of commits, finished tasks, notes, follow-ups and focus sessions.
  • Now and Up next keep today in view, without the clutter.
  • Wrap up the day and copy a worklog for any date range.

Private by design

No account, no server, no analytics. Your data stays on your devices and in your own private iCloud. On Mac, Sill reads only git history — never your code. Read the privacy policy.
