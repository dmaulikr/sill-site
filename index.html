Questions about Sill

What is Sill?

Sill is an app for software developers on Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It writes your daily standup, with Yesterday, Today and Blockers, from the work you already do: your git commits on the Mac, the tasks you finish and the follow-ups you log. One tap copies it.

Does Sill read my code?

No. On the Mac it reads local git commit history, meaning commit messages and times, never source code. Commits stay on the Mac and don't sync.

How does blocker tracking work?

For each blocker, such as an API, a design, a review or access, Sill shows how long it has been open and how many times you've followed up, and adds that to your standup.

Is Sill free?

Yes, including the set-time standup reminder. Sill Pro is optional and adds the review generator, calendar-based reminders, unlimited blockers and a worklog for any date range.

Is my data private?

There's no account, no server and no analytics. Your data stays on your devices and in your private iCloud.

Which devices does it work on?

Mac with macOS 14 or later, iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, and Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later.
